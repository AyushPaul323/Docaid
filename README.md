Doc+Aid
Doc+Aid is an all-in-one healthcare service platform designed to simplify and enhance the patient experience through digital transformation. The project focuses on streamlining essential healthcare services such as hospital bed booking, doctor consultations, ambulance assistance, and secure medical record storage.

🚀 Features
🏥 Hospital Bed Availability & Booking
Check real-time availability of hospital beds.

Book beds instantly at nearby hospitals.

Reduce delays during medical emergencies.

🧑‍⚕️ Doctor-Patient Consultancy
Connect with doctors through virtual (video/audio) or offline consultations.

Improved and faster communication between doctors and patients.

Appointment scheduling and reminders.

🚑 Ambulance Booking with OTP Verification
Request emergency ambulance services instantly.

OTP-based verification ensures secure and reliable response.

Location-based ambulance dispatch system.

📁 Secure Medical Record Storage
Patients can store prescriptions, diagnosis reports, and medical history.

Access records anytime via their account dashboard.

Ensures privacy, accessibility, and centralized health data.

🧠 Objective
The goal of Doc+Aid is to build a digital healthcare bridge that connects patients to critical services efficiently, especially in emergency situations. The platform offers a seamless user experience and ensures that help is always just a few clicks away.

🔧 Technologies Used
Frontend:  ReactJs 

Backend: Node.js 

Database: Firebase

Authentication & Security:  OTP-based verification

APIs: Maps API, SMS/Email API for notifications

📦 Future Enhancements
Integration with government health schemes.

Real-time hospital capacity management.

AI-based health recommendations and chatbot support.
